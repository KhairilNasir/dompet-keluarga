            Get started in 2 minutes

            No code, no Apps Script. Just a Google Sheet and one link.

            1. Open sheets.new to create a new, empty Google Sheet. Name it anything, e.g. Family Budget.
            2. Click Share at the top right. Add the email shown in Settings as an Editor and click Send. This lets the app write your records into the sheet.
            3. In the same Share window, click Copy link.
            4. Come back here, open Settings, paste the link and click Connect. A green Connected label means you're done.
            5. Go to Monthly and start adding your salary, commitments and savings.

            Connect your partner

            1. In Settings, click Copy link for partner.
            2. Send that link to your husband or wife on WhatsApp.
            3. When they open it, their phone connects to the same sheet. Whatever one of you adds, the other sees.
            4. Want your partner to open the Google Sheet too? Share the sheet with their email as well.

            Don't post the partner link in a group chat. Anyone with that link can see your records.

            See your records in Google Sheets

            1. In Settings, click Open Google Sheet, or find it in Google Drive.
            2. Records tab: every record, one row each. You can also type rows here directly.
            3. Summary tab: totals per month. Change the year in the yellow cell B1.

            Install the Google Sheets app on your phone to check anytime.

            FAQ

            It says "Share this sheet with … as Editor first"

            The app can't reach your sheet yet. Open the sheet → Share → add the email shown in Settings as Editor → Send. Then click Connect again.

            It says "Please use a new, empty Google Sheet"

            For safety, the app only connects to a blank sheet (or one it set up before). Create a new one at sheets.new and try again.

            Can other families see my records?

            No. Every family has its own sheet. The admin who set up this site can technically open the sheets shared with them, so only use a site run by someone you trust.

            I changed phone or browser. Are my records gone?

            No. Your records live in your Google Sheet. Paste the sheet link in Settings again, or ask your partner for the partner link.

            Can I use it without a Google Sheet?

            Yes, but records are only saved in that browser, your partner can't see them, and they disappear if the browser data is cleared.

            How is the financial health level calculated?

            It looks at your debt ratio (loan and credit card payments ÷ income; limit 40%), savings rate (target 20%), the 50/30/20 guide (needs / wants / savings) and whether you have a balance or a deficit. This is general guidance, not professional financial advice.
